# Luke 作品集

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Confirmed: React, TypeScript, Vite and Three.js. Static deployment; no application backend.

## Users
Recruiters and potential collaborators evaluating Luke's product judgement, implementation ability and creative work. Chinese is the primary language. Desktop first, with a complete touch experience.

## Product Purpose
Let visitors discover eight real projects through an interactive lotus pond, follow a selected koi into a horizontal work collection, and open a project into a readable vertical case study.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Eight bounded, continuously wandering koi represent the eight supplied projects. They avoid each other and are often obscured by dense leaves.
- Pointer or touch motion parts leaves. Selecting a visible koi takes the visitor to the corresponding project in the adjacent horizontal collection.
- The selected koi remains the same object during the transition and guides horizontal browsing, sometimes circling a leaf.
- Project leaves initially contain only a name and an original project emblem; activation opens the full case study.
- All visual subjects and media previews use real dithering. Warm paper grain is exempt. A clear screenshot view is explicitly permitted.
- Project claims must be grounded in supplied material and public source. Unverified metrics and sole-authorship claims are omitted.

## Brand Commitments
Oblique pond, overlapping ink-black and grey leaves, muted pink-violet lotus flowers, warm vermilion/gold/ivory koi, yellow xuan-paper ground. No large introductory headline or biography on the pond.

## Evidence on Hand
The original `各项目亮点.docx`, 23 supplied images and one 73-second video under `screenshots for use/`, plus eight public GitHub repositories. Source material is immutable.

## Product Principles
- The work leads; discovery feels alive and unhurried.
- Explain design decisions through real product evidence.
- Preserve motion continuity and predictable navigation.
- Keep meaningful text semantic and readable.
- Build and inspect a live visual sample before expanding the full scene.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard project activation, touch leaf-parting, focus restoration, reduced motion and a non-WebGL fallback must preserve access to all projects.
